Roles of people:
Milk box stock milk and juice in fridge. just need their time in and out and lunch. Closing milkbox shift (3-11) takes lunch at 7pm
closing cashier (3-11) takes lunch 6:30-7pm

Courtesy Clerks. Do the same things as GMCs. schedule them for everything

janitor. No gobacks or carts. only sweeps whole shift

GMCs. carts sweeps gobacks.
general rule, whoever has sweeps does go backs at the same time.

"Utility clerk" is the closing milkbox shift. First three hours from 3-6pm just bags

9 hour shifts get a hour lunch, else half hour

"Keys", just need time in and out

Back end GMCs time in/time out. no sweeps or carts

front end GMCs do sweeps and carts

CSRs "in charge" opening CSR for morning, CSR who's off the latest in the evening

Opening GMC/Courtesy clerk has carts and sweeps at the same time for the first hour.
Closing GMC/Courtesy clerk has carts and sweeps for the last hour.

Breaks at 10 minutes, first break is two hours in, lunch is four hours in.
last break is 10 minutes after lunch ends.

Shifts over five or six hours, I forget. get a lunch and two breaks.

shifts under five hours just get one break.
